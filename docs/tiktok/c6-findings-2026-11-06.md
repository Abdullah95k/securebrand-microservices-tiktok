## 2. Correction: posts carry no `locationCreated` in TikTok's web answers

Several documents call `locationCreated`, the country a post was made in, "the strongest Iraq signal" and say the account's post list carries it. That includes `discovery-plan.md` §3, ADR 0007's Iraq score, the discovery runbook, and tks's answers to the master (field inventory, contract review). **No web answer tks has ever measured carries it:**

| Source | Posts | With `locationCreated` |
|---|---|---|
| The owner's recordings from Iraq (2026-10-31): the field list of all 253 posts | 253 | 0 (not in the list) |
| The probe's fixtures (C2, C3, US exits) and the recordings' fixtures | 30 | 0 |
| C6 run 1 (creator lists of the owner's accounts, reposts) | 106 | 0 |
| C6 run 2 (hashtags, sounds, related, feed, 30 creator lists) | 195 raw, 396 read | 0 |
| Search results in the owner's recording of 2026-11-04 | — | 0 (already noted) |

The parser reads the field (`internal/tt/parse/posts.go`), from public sources about TikTok's app API, but the web never sends it.

**What this changes:**
1. **The Iraq score.** Its strong signal "a post made in Iraq" never fires. Accounts still qualify on the other strong signals:
   - an Iraqi place or the Kurdistan Region in the bio or name;
   - a `+964` phone number;
   - a `.iq` link;
   - Iraqi places in captions of 3 of the posts read;
   - 10 audience signals.

   **The thresholds should be checked with the owner during testing**, since one strong signal fewer means fewer automatic passes (`tt.discovery.score`; T39).
2. **The shared contract.** `Post.created_in_country` stays a valid field. tks will leave it unset (absent, not empty).
3. **tks's earlier answers** to the master are wrong on this point (the inventory's "region: often", the contract review's "strongest Iraq signal"). The master is told.
